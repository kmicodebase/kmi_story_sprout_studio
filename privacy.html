StorySprout Studio privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · StorySprout Studio is run by Kindness Matters Inc · Contact: kmi.aistorybooks@gmail.com

StorySprout Studio is a web app where children write and illustrate picture books with Pip, an AI illustrator, and teachers collect their class's work. This page covers the Studio (kmicodebase.github.io/kmi_story_sprout_studio) and its teacher pages. It says what we collect, why, and who else handles it.

Children

  • Children do not make accounts or sign in, and we do not ask for their names or any contact details.
  • A story is saved in the child's own browser and in a file on the computer they use.
  • On a class computer, the story is encrypted in the browser before it is sent. Our storage holds only that encrypted copy and cannot read it. The only readable copy goes to the teacher's own computer.
  • To draw a picture, the words a child gives Pip and the story text are sent to OpenAI, which makes the picture and Pip's replies. They are sent through our server, which does not keep them.

Teachers who sign in with Google

  • Google tells us your name, email address and a Google account id. We ask for nothing else from your Google account: no contacts, no files, no calendar.
  • When you create a teacher account you also give us your name, school, the school's ZIP code and, if you choose, the grades you teach. We store this with your email address and the dates you signed up and were approved for printed books.
  • We use it to run your account: to know which class teams are yours, to decide whether printed books are switched on for you, and to contact you about your account or your orders. When you sign up, StorySprout is sent an email with these details.
  • Your class teams are recorded with a scrambled form of your Google account id, not your name or email.
  • We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services listed below that run the Studio for us.

Printed book orders

  • An order includes the book, the number of copies, the class's grades and size, and the shipping name, address and phone number you enter.
  • It is stored with your account and emailed to StorySprout, who place it with a print shop. The print shop receives the book and the shipping details.

Class survey

After a school network has made many requests to Pip, the Studio asks one short survey: school name, class size, grade and whether it is an after-school program. To know when to ask, we count requests per network using a scrambled form of the network address, not the address itself.

Services that handle this for us

  • Google: teacher sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: runs our server and stores accounts, orders, survey answers and the encrypted class work.
  • OpenAI: makes Pip's pictures and replies from what is sent to it.
  • Resend: sends the sign-up and order emails to StorySprout.
  • GitHub Pages: serves the web pages.

Keeping and deleting

  • A teacher can delete a class team, and everything stored for it, from the teacher page at any time.
  • To delete your teacher account, or ask what we hold about you, email kmi.aistorybooks@gmail.com. Deleting an account also deletes every class team it owns. We keep records of printed book orders already sent.
  • Work saved on your own computer is yours, and we cannot reach or delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
