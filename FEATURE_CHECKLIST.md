# Feature checklist (16 October 2026)

Transcribed from `Story_Sprout_Team_Task_Checklist-2.pdf`, which was deleted once
this copy was saved. Wording is the original's.

## Send only student changes to the teacher

After the initial book transfer, send only changed text, images, and page information to
the teacher. Avoid resending unchanged images or the entire book for a small edit. Keep
the teacher copy current when pages are added, deleted, or reordered and when students
reconnect.

## Integrate the teacher workspace into Studio

Make the teacher page accessible within Studio, including teacher sign-in, class setup,
team codes, and collection controls. Teachers can open and review complete student
books and return to the student workspace without losing unsaved work.

## Physical book printing and delivery

Connect physical book ordering to Staples and mail the printed books to the entered
delivery address. Collect the required recipient name, mailing address, school name,
student grades, and class size only as part of print checkout. Reuse entered details to
keep repeat orders simple.

Each physical copy ordered counts as one usage, including a revised version of a book.
Failed jobs do not count. No order-cancellation option is provided.

## Lifetime printing allowances

Give each signed-in teacher an initial allowance of 3 books, tracked for the lifetime of that
teacher-sign-on and school combination. When the 3-book quota runs out, prompt for
additional information as part of requesting more books and automatically increase the
allowance to 6 books total.

For more than 6 books, provide an email option to arrange an increase. Apply a hard
lifetime limit of 10 books across the entire school, including all teachers and classes.
Email-arranged increases remain subject to that school limit. Show used and remaining
allowances; retain usage across devices and sign-ins.

## One-time prompt after 1,000 requests

When the total request count for an IP range exceeds 1,000, display the prompt once. If a
teacher is signed in and the app is in teacher/student mode, show the prompt to the
teacher. Outside teacher/student mode, show it to the student. Remember that the
prompt has been shown so it does not keep appearing.

## F01 - Reliable saving and recovery

Clearly distinguish local autosave, downloaded project files, and transfer to the teacher.
Show pending or failed saves accurately. When saved work is available after a reload or
reopening, offer to resume or recover it instead of starting with a blank book.

## F02 - Whole-book preview and PDF export

Preview and export the complete book with all pages in order, readable text, correctly
positioned images, and correct page boundaries. Keep the existing PNG export option.
PDF export and local printing do not consume the physical-book allowance.

## F04 - Image comparison and history

Make the Image Bank, earlier image versions, and comparison controls easy to find. Let
children compare alternatives, reject a new image, and restore an earlier image without
losing work. Generating or selecting an illustration must leave the child's story text
unchanged.
